Delete Your MyTripPilot Account

Request deletion of your MyTripPilot account and associated data.

What will be deleted?

When your account deletion request is completed, MyTripPilot will delete the personal information and application data associated with your account, subject to any information that we are legally required or legitimately permitted to retain.

This may include:

  • Your MyTripPilot account
  • Email address associated with your account
  • Trips created by you
  • Expense records associated with your account
  • Expense split and settlement information
  • Packing checklist information
  • Trip cover images uploaded by you
  • Your membership of trips you joined
  • Other personal information associated with your account

Trips you created are deleted for all of their members. Expenses, settlements, and participant names you added to trips owned by other people remain in those trips so that the other members' records stay accurate, but they are no longer linked to your account. Reports you submitted are kept for up to 12 months for moderation and safety purposes without a link to your account.

Important:

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Please make sure that you have exported any trip or expense information that you want to keep before requesting deletion.

How to Request Account Deletion

Option 1 — In-app deletion (recommended):

Open the MyTripPilot app → tap ⚙️ Account in the top-right corner → tap Delete Account. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

Option 2 — Email request (if you cannot access the app):

Send an email from the email address associated with your MyTripPilot account to:

support@mytrippilot.in

Please use the subject line: MyTripPilot Account Deletion Request

We may request reasonable information to verify that the request was submitted by the account owner before processing the deletion. We will process your request within 7 days of verification.

Our reply, including any verification questions, will come from mytrippilot@gmail.com.

Processing of Your Request

After receiving and verifying your request, we will process the account deletion in accordance with our data deletion procedures.

For both options, personal data associated exclusively with your account is removed from our active systems within 7 days.

If certain information must be retained for legitimate reasons, such as security, fraud prevention, legal, or regulatory requirements, the applicable information will be retained only for the required period.
